# Reportband.gov portal

## Process on reportband.gov

1. Start, click continue.
    1. Can continue in french and spanish
2. Who, Please select all the options that apply to you
    1. I am the finder or am reporting on behalf of the finder
    2. I am a bird bander or a bander’s assistant
    3. I am federal, state, or provincial official
3. What, I am reporting a:
    1. Federal band (metal band with 8 or 9 digits).
    2. Federal band and color marker from the same bird.
    3. Color marker only (neck collar, wing tag, colored leg band, ect.).
4. About Band, Bird, and Date
    1. Federal Band Number [Prefix-Suffix]
    2. There was a second federal band on this bird
    3. There was a reward band on this bird
    4. Do you know the species? [Yes][No]
    5. How obtained? [Shot.][Found dead.][Caught or found dead due to: control operations (roost bombing,gassing, avicides, wetting agents, ect.)][Saw or photographed neck collar, color band, or other marker (not federal band) while bird was free][Saw or photographed federal band while bird was free][Hit by motor vehicle or found dead or injured on road][Caught due to: injury][Previously banded burd trapped and released during banding operations][Caught uninjured bird by hand][Purchased, eBay, traded or received band from someone else][Found band or band number only (including metal detecting)]
    6. See more options [Choose one (or explain in comments)][Trapped or snared (not for banding operation)][Entangled in fishing gear][Struck building or window][Captured or collected for scientific project (not for banding operations)][Banding mortality][Caught due to disease][Other(please explain in comments)]
    7. Condition of the band [Removed from Bird][Left on Bird][Unknown][Please select a band condition]
    8. Condition of the bird [Dead][Alive Released][Alive In Captivity][Alive][Unknown][Please select a bird condition]
    9. Date of encounter [mm/dd/yyyy]
    10. Comments (optional)
5. Color Marker Info
    1. Click “Continue” to continue
    2. If bird did have an auxilary marker click here
6. Encounter location
    1. Country
    2. State/Providence

    1. First Name

    3. Email

    5. Country
    6. Street Address
    7. Zip/Postal Code
    8. City
    9. State/Providence
    10. Would you like to recieve a certificate of the banding data via email [Yes][No]
